# Hebrew AI

An index of the people, organisations, models, datasets and benchmarks improving support for **modern Hebrew** across AI tasks, from large language model adaptations to small specialist models such as nikud (diacritisation).

In short: Hebrew-language AI, wherever it happens. Israeli AI in general is out of scope: Israel is the natural centre of Hebrew-language AI, but an AI company that just happens to be based in Israel doesn't belong here; Israel-focused AI is indexed in [Israeli-AI](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Israeli-AI). Full definition: [SCOPE.md](SCOPE.md).

**Last updated:** November 18, 2026
