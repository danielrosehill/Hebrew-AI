# Scope: Hebrew AI

**Hebrew AI** here means the people, organisations, models and datasets improving support for **modern Hebrew** across AI tasks, from large language model adaptations to small specialist models such as nikud (diacritisation).

This repo is the hub: it links out to sub-indexes, each with its own SCOPE.md.

## In scope

- Hebrew-specialist models: LLM adaptations, ASR, TTS, OCR, NLP (NER, sentiment, morphology, nikud), embeddings, translation ([Hebrew-AI-Models](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Hebrew-AI-Models)).
- Notable multilingual models and datasets with real Hebrew support, kept in separate subsections from the Hebrew-specialist ones.
- Hebrew-language datasets: speech and audio, sentence, text corpora, instruction, parallel text, task datasets ([Hebrew-Datasets](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Hebrew-Datasets)).
- People and organisations doing Hebrew-language AI work, wherever they are based ([Hebrew-AI-Community](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Hebrew-AI-Community)).
- Benchmarks, leaderboards and evaluations of Hebrew-language AI.
- English-Hebrew code-switching (English speech or text with Hebrew words), as a Hebrew-language AI problem.

## Out of scope

- **Israeli AI in general.** Israel is the only country where modern Hebrew is an official language, so it is the natural centre of activity, and many entries here are Israeli. But an AI company, project or person that just happens to be based in Israel is out of scope: that list is far too large, and this set is about Hebrew-language AI specifically. Israel-focused AI is indexed separately in [Israeli-AI](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Israeli-AI).
- Biblical, rabbinic or Paleo-Hebrew work is in scope only where it is AI work on the language (a model, dataset or tool), not general Jewish-studies software.
